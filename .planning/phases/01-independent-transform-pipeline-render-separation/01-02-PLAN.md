  <files>
    native-windows/src/Engine/Animation/PropertyBinder.cs
    native-windows/src/UI/StudioView.cs
  </files>
  <action>
    Per D-02, D-04, D-05, and D-08 in CONTEXT.md:
    1. In `PropertyBinder.ApplyPoseToLayer`:
       - Do NOT mutate `layer.Width` or `layer.Height` when scaling!
       - Read `PropKey.ScaleX` and `PropKey.ScaleY` from pose and assign to `layer.RenderTransform.ScaleX` and `ScaleY`.
       - Assign translation offsets to `layer.RenderTransform.TranslationX` and `TranslationY` (or preserve `layer.X`/`Y` design origin).
       - Assign rotation to `layer.RenderTransform.Rotation`.
       - If `layer.RenderTransform` is null, instantiate it on demand.
    2. In `StudioView.cs`:
       - Refactor `ApplyAnimationPose` to update `layer.RenderTransform` instead of mutating `layer.Width`, `layer.Height`, or rewriting child bounds.
       - Deprecate destructive `BezierVectorEngine.ScaleGeometry` calls during animated parent transform propagation.
       - Ensure `RestoreAnimationRestPose` / `ClearAnimationTransforms` resets `RenderTransform` to identity cleanly without corrupting design canvas rest state.
  </action>
  <verify>
    Run `cd native-windows; .\build.bat` to confirm clean compilation.
  </verify>
  <done>
    Animation playback evaluates into `RenderTransform`, leaving `LayerNode.Width`, `LayerNode.Height`, and vector geometry intact.
  </done>
